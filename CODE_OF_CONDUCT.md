# Code of conduct

UISet adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project
gathers.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, critique code rather than people,
and keep harassment, insults and personal attacks out of every space the project uses.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes the project unwelcoming, contact the maintainer, superzero11,
privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in
confidence, and the maintainer will respond within a week.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code,
and may ban people from the project, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.
